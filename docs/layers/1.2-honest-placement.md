# Layer 1.2 — Honest membership + exclusive function placement

**Status:** Fabric Bootstrap (corrective)  
**Depends on:** 1.1 cluster autodiscovery

## Problem

1.1 advertised every mDNS peer as if it could `execute` / `schedule`, and `omls nodes` labeled LAN sightings as `ready`. That made discovery look like real division of work across computers when peers had not joined the fabric or executed anything.

## What landed

- Operator-facing node status is now `joined` (fabric-registered and available), `discovered` (seen on the LAN only), or `unavailable`.
- `omls status` prints `joined=` and `discovered=` counts.
- mDNS TXT capability is `discover` only — execute/schedule require a fabric join.
- `run-demo` reports `planned` vs `executed` workers/hosts. A shortfall is called out in the policy note.
- Policy `place` (alias `exclusive`) assigns distinct functions (`parallel_workers`, `memory_touch`) to different nodes when two or more fabric-joined machines exist, and refuses to pretend they ran elsewhere.

## Out of scope

Arbitrary user binaries (`ffmpeg`, etc.), remote RAM/GPU sharing, and enterprise job queues.
